About VGM_Editz

A one-person edit shop focused on short-form video that's built to be rewatched.

I love the art of editing — VGM_Editz is where I share it.

My edits sit across superhero and Marvel moments, gaming highlights, viral memes, anime shorts, and phonk-scored cuts — always chasing the same thing: rhythm and flow that make a clip feel inevitable, not assembled.

How An Edit Gets Made

From Clip To Cut

The pass every edit runs through before it goes up on the channel.

Source & sync

Footage gets pulled together and laid against the track — the beat decides where every cut lands.

Build in After Effects

Motion, transitions, and effects are built out in AE to give the edit its shape and pace.

Upscale with Topaz AI

Footage gets cleaned up and upscaled so it holds up sharp on a vertical screen.

Finish & export

Final polish runs through Premiere Pro, CapCut, or Alight Motion depending on what the clip needs, then it ships.
